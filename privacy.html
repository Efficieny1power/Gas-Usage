Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

Welcome to Smart Usage ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Smart Usage is designed as a privacy-first, offline-capable mobile application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data, analytics, or telemetry to remote servers.

2. Local Data Storage

All information generated while using the app—including your installation names, cylinder sizes, exchange history, refill prices, appliance maintenance schedules, and attached Gas Certificates of Compliance (CoC) PDFs—is stored strictly and securely on your local device using internal storage and browser local storage.

3. Pro Subscriptions & Payments

Pro subscriptions and simulated in-app purchases are handled securely. No sensitive financial or credit card data is ever stored on our systems.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@smartusage.gasmonitor.com.
